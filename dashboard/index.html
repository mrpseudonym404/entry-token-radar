Method. Implied value = TVL × M_cat × G × B × A. M_cat is the category multiple, shrunk toward the global median: M_cat = (n × category median + 5 × global median) / (n + 5), k = 5, where n is the number of tokenized peers in the category. A category with few peers is pulled toward the global median 0.1689 instead of trusting a thin median. The old flat 0.50 haircut was dropped — its conservatism now lives in the shrinkage. G rewards 7-day TVL momentum, B rewards breadth across chains, A peaks at 1–2 years and falls for very old protocols. Farm plans are generated from each protocol's own record, not financial advice. The 20 rankable protocols above the $50M floor are not on the board: the house watchlist drops them for reasons outside the snapshot's ten fields (a parent protocol), so they are conservatively held out.
